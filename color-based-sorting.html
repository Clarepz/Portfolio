Images

Train an imitation-learning policy to pick and place objects according to their color. It is the second task of the Real-World Robotics course at ETH Zurich (HS26), and it is still in progress.

The task has three parts: collecting an extensive dataset of demonstrations, setting up the imitation-learning training and training the policy, and deploying the policy in the real world. It is scored by the number of successful relocations within a given time.
